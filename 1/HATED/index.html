03 / THE STORY

WHAT
HAPPENED
BECAME
HER.

“You saw her. You still don't know her.”

HATED begins after the explanation has already failed. It is the sound of someone who has spent too long carrying other people's judgement — and finally decides that being misunderstood is not the same as being defeated.

LAIYA's debut stays deliberately mysterious. The visuals hide as much as they reveal: messy hair, hard light, cropped eyes, hands, movement, shadow and distance. Before the audience knows her face, they know her feeling.

The anger is not presented as weakness. It is the evidence that something mattered. The song turns that anger into movement, then into a voice.
