01 / THE DEBUT ERA

FIVE
SONGS.
ONE
STORY.

HATED is only the beginning.

The first mini album is designed as a gradual reveal. Track one introduces the anger. The songs that follow widen the story — moving from confrontation to memory, identity, refusal and finally the person behind the voice.

The campaign should not reveal everything at once. Every release adds another piece of LAIYA.

PHASE 01
HATED

The first statement. Establish the voice and visual language.

PHASE 02
HELLO

First crack in the mystery. A new emotional angle.

PHASE 03
LUKA

Expand the world with character and memory.

PHASE 04–05
NO / SHE

Final chapters leading into the complete mini album.

02 / CAMPAIGN

DON'T
SHOW
EVERYTHING.

Mystery is part of the identity.

For the debut era, keep LAIYA's face partially hidden, cropped or turned away. Let wardrobe, hands, silhouette, movement and monochrome photography become recognisable before the audience gets a complete portrait.

03 / VISUAL LANGUAGE

BLACK.
WHITE.
ACID.

A visual system strong enough to carry five releases.

Keep the campaign predominantly black and white. Use acid green only as a signal: available music, future release, an underline, a flash of light. The colour becomes synonymous with the LAIYA debut era.

04 / INNER CIRCLE

BE
THERE
FIRST.

Give new listeners a reason to stay.

Collect email sign-ups for release announcements, exclusive visuals, pre-save links and mini-album news. Connect the form to your email service before launch.
