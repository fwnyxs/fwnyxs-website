02 / Photoshoot direction

THE
HATED
PORTRAIT.

Concept: anti-glamour editorial. Shoot LAIYA in a bare concrete/interior location using hard direct flash, deep shadows, slight motion blur and cropped frames. Avoid polished pop-star posing.

Wardrobe: black, washed white, charcoal, worn denim, leather or utilitarian pieces. One recognisable signature item should recur across the campaign.

Image language: 35mm-style grain, photocopy texture, contact sheets, close-ups of eyes/hands/mouth, negative space, occasional overexposure.

SHOT 01

THE GLARE

Direct-to-camera portrait. Hard flash. No smile. Crop aggressively so the face almost becomes graphic texture.

SHOT 02

THE BLUR

Longer shutter / movement. LAIYA turns away while the camera catches one sharp detail. Emotional rather than glamorous.

SHOT 03

THE VOID

Full-body image surrounded by negative space. Tiny figure, huge frame. Ideal for posters, streaming headers and the website.
