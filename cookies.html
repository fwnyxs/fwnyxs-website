COOKIES.

Last updated: 24 August 2026.

What this covers

This page explains the cookies and similar storage (like your browser's local storage) that fwnyxs.my uses, and how you can control them.

What we use today

FWNYXS does not currently run advertising or tracking cookies. The only thing stored right now is your cookie preference itself, saved in your browser so we don't ask you again on every visit.

If that changes

If we add analytics in future to understand how the site is used, it will only run if you choose "Accept All" in the cookie banner. Choosing "Necessary Only" keeps analytics off.

Your choice

You can change your preference at any time using the button below.
